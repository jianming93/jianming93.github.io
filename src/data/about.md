## About me

I'm a Senior Data Scientist at GoTo, working on pricing for ride hailing. What keeps me hooked on machine learning and data science is how wide the craft is: I enjoy the algorithms and the maths behind a model just as much as the engineering it takes to run it reliably in production.

That curiosity has taken me across the stack. At EM2AI I built and deployed computer vision models for dental X-rays, and designed a clinical decision support system using a rule-based decision tree and a knowledge graph. At DSTA I moved closer to the engineering side, building LLM and retrieval augmented generation (RAG) platform tooling and a machine learning inference platform on OpenShift, and optimising model serving with Triton Inference Server.

I'm keeping this learning log to work through the LLM stack properly, in public: from the algorithms down to GPU kernels and serving.
