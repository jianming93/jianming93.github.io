Learning has always been a constant for me. Data science, machine learning and
AI change so quickly that the learning never stops, even when working
professionally. And even when my path leads somewhere slightly adjacent, I
still want to know what is going on out there, simply because the field itself
is fun and exciting.

There are many ways to keep notes (Notion, Obsidian, etc.), but I wanted
something that does more than store them. Writing in public also trains me
to communicate more clearly, and explaining a topic is also a way of testing
my understanding of a topic itself. In that sense, this log is an accompaniment
to my own second brain itself.
